# Malaysia Veterinary Industry Research

**Date compiled:** 10 July 2026
**Scope:** Malaysian veterinary industry, with primary focus on companies that **import and stock veterinary medicines/animal health products** (distributors, wholesalers, MNC subsidiaries) rather than clinics themselves. Clinic-side data is included as demand-side context.

**Method note:** Compiled from ~30 web searches across regulatory (.gov.my), company, news, and market-research sources. Direct fetches of dvs.gov.my and npra.gov.my PDFs were blocked at network level, so gov.my figures are relayed via search-indexed snippets, not primary-document review — flagged inline where relevant. No company names or figures below are fabricated; anything with thin sourcing is explicitly marked as unverified.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

- Malaysia's veterinary/animal health market is **small, fragmented, and split across two demand pools**: companion animal (pet care) and livestock/production animal (poultry, swine, cattle, aquaculture) — the latter is arguably the larger commercial opportunity given Malaysia's ~1.8 billion broiler/year poultry industry.
- **Regulation of imports runs through two agencies**: NPRA (pharmaceuticals/biologics, under Poisons Act 1952 + Control of Drugs and Cosmetics Regulations 1984) and DVS (vaccines, feed additives, diagnostic kits, import permits under Animal Act 1953). There is no single "one-stop" registrar — this bifurcation itself is a structural friction point for new entrants.
- **Only one Bursa-listed pure-play**: Rhone Ma Holdings Berhad (RHONEMA), an end-to-end distributor/manufacturer, posted RM211.6M FY2024 revenue with animal health products/equipment segment profit up 62% YoY — the closest thing to a public benchmark for what a scaled player looks like.
- **Distribution is dominated by a handful of named importers/distributors** (Rhone Ma, Pahang Pharmacy Animal Health, Y.S.P. Industries, Range Pharma, Asia Veterinary, Vemedim, Kuga Vet Enterprise) plus MNC subsidiaries (Zoetis, MSD Animal Health, Boehringer Ingelheim, Elanco, Ceva) that mostly operate direct entities for livestock/pharma but route companion-animal products through local distributors.
- **No PE-backed or foreign clinic-consolidation platform exists in Malaysia** (unlike Singapore/Australia's VetPartners or the US's Mars Veterinary Health/IVC Evidensia) — the ~650 registered clinics are independently owned. This is a notable white-space signal, though it sits downstream of the import/distribution focus of this report.
- **Market-size estimates disagree by ~4x** depending on scope (USD 117M pure animal-health-products vs USD 450M when services are folded in) — treat any single figure with caution.

## 2. Regulatory Framework (Who Governs Imports)

| Body | Governs | Legal basis | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NPRA** (National Pharmaceutical Regulatory Agency) | Veterinary pharmaceuticals, biologics, medicated feed additives | Control of Drugs and Cosmetics Regulations 1984; adjudicated by the Drug Control Authority (DCA) | Registration guideline: REGOVP. Foreign manufacturers must appoint a **local authorized agent** — this is the entry point for a distribution business model. |
| **DVS** (Department of Veterinary Services / Jabatan Perkhidmatan Veterinar) | Import permits for live animals/animal products; registers vaccines, feed additives, supplements, pet food, diagnostic test kits | Animal Act 1953 (s.84 import permission for live disease agents/vaccines; s.30 license to possess/administer) | Also the SPS (sanitary/phytosanitary) competent authority. |
| **Poisons Act 1952 (Act 366)** | Classifies veterinary drugs (mostly Group C poisons) | — | Registered vets may supply/prescribe; underlies the prescription requirement seen at online pet pharmacies. |
| **Malaysian Veterinary Council (MVC)** | Practice regulation (registers vets, sets ethics) — **not** import/distribution | Veterinary Surgeons Act 1974 | Malaysia's equivalent of a veterinary practice board. |

**Public registers that exist** (URLs confirmed, contents not independently pulled — recommend direct review):
- NPRA **QUEST3+** — searchable product/license database, includes "Poison Veterinary" / "Non Poison Veterinary" classes: https://quest3plus.bpfk.gov.my/pmo2/index.php
- DVS **List of Registered Veterinary Products** (Jan 2019): https://www.dvs.gov.my/dvs/resources/user_1/2020/KAV/LIST-OF-REGISTERED-VETERINARY-PRODUCTS-JAN-2019.pdf
- DVS **List of Approved Veterinary Vaccines** (May 2018): https://www.dvs.gov.my/dvs/resources/user_1/2020/KAV/List_of_Approved_Veterinary_Vaccine_updated_May_2018_posted_in_web_dvs.pdf
- DVS **List of Registered Veterinary Diagnostic Test Kits** (Jul 2020): https://www.dvs.gov.my/dvs/resources/user_1/2020/KAV/LIST_OF_REGISTERED_VETERINARY_DIAGNOSTIC_TEST_KITS_(July_2020).pdf

**Action item if this is pursued further:** these DVS/NPRA lists are the actual "how many players" answer — a full company-by-company count of registered veterinary product license-holders. They need to be opened directly in a browser (the research agent's fetch was blocked at the sandbox network layer, not by the government site itself).

## 5. Demand-Side Context

### 5.1 Companion animal / clinic landscape
- **~650 registered veterinary clinics nationwide**; 61 newly licensed in the most recent reporting period — market is still growing.
- **~2,600 active practicing veterinarians** against an estimated need of 8,500 (DVS, Apr 2026) — a shortfall of ~5,900. Vet-to-population ratio ~1:13,700 vs. 1:5,000 international benchmark.
- **No PE-backed or foreign consolidator present.** Largest identified chains are small, independently owned (2–7 branches): iVET Petcare, Vet Partners (M) Sdn Bhd, Healing Pets Group, Ohana Veterinary Clinic, Vets for Pets Animal Clinic, Petcare Enterprise. This contrasts sharply with Singapore/Australia (VetPartners, EQT-owned, 267 clinics) and the US/Europe (Mars Veterinary Health, IVC Evidensia).
- Pet ownership estimated 51–65% of households (sources disagree); ~3.5M dogs, ~2.8M cats (unverified secondary figure).
- Online pet-medicine channels exist but require prescriptions per Pharmacy Board rules: Pet Lovers Centre's "Pet Pharmacy," and Vet Pharmacy (Vulcan Post-covered). No Chewy/PetCareRx-scale platform exists yet.

### 5.2 Livestock / production animal
- **Poultry**: >1.8 billion broilers/year, ~near-100% self-sufficiency, ~2,300 poultry breeders. This is the largest volume driver for animal health products in Malaysia.
- **Swine**: Declining sharply — 1.67M (2021) → 1.24M pigs (2024), a 25% drop, driven by African Swine Fever, feed costs, biosecurity tightening. Pork self-sufficiency fell from 84.6% to under 70%.
- **Cattle**: Government pushing feedlot integration with palm oil estates (via MPOB, RISDA, FELCRA), targeting 50% beef self-sufficiency by 2030.
- **Aquaculture**: 1.89M metric tonnes production (2022, RM15.97B), declining due to disease (e.g., shrimp Early Mortality Syndrome); animal-health/AMR monitoring in this segment described as "still scarce" — a potential gap.
- Livestock access to vet medicine runs largely through **DVS extension services + feed companies/cooperatives** (Cargill, Gold Coin Feedmills, Leong Hup, MFM, FFM) rather than direct veterinary clinics. Malaysian Feedmillers Association members produce ~55% of national feed. Animal feed sales: RM16.49B (2021).

## 7. Key Takeaways for a "How Many Players" Answer

Based on named, sourced companies (excludes clinics, which are a separate ~650-count figure):

- **1 Bursa-listed pure-play**: Rhone Ma Holdings Berhad
- **~7-8 larger/verified importer-distributors**: Rhone Ma, PPAH, Y.S.P. Industries, Range Pharma, Asia Veterinary, Shennong, Vemedim, Kuga Vet Enterprise
- **~4 smaller companion-animal distributors**: Progenesis, Growel, Avantpet, Nam Pharma (lower confidence)
- **~5 MNCs with direct Malaysia subsidiaries**: Zoetis, MSD Animal Health, Boehringer Ingelheim, Elanco, Ceva
- **~4 MNCs operating via local distributor/agent only** (no direct entity): Virbac, Vetoquinol, Merial legacy brands, Bayer (absorbed into Elanco)

This gives a working count of **roughly 15-20 identifiable import/distribution players**, which is almost certainly an undercount of the *true* universe — the DVS and NPRA public registers (Section 2) are the only way to get an exhaustive, authoritative number, since product registration requires a named local licensee/agent for every SKU. That is the natural next step if a precise count is needed.
