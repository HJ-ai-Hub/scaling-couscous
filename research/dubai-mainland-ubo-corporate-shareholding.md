# Dubai Mainland (DET) — Corporate Shareholding & UBO Declaration: Research & Standard Format

**Prepared:** 2026-06-14
**Scope:** New **mainland LLC** licensed by the **Dubai Department of Economy & Tourism (DET, formerly DED)**, owned by **one UAE‑registered corporate shareholder + two individuals**.
**Purpose:** Establish the facts (legal requirements + standard document format) so the actual declarations can be drafted once the shareholding structure is provided.

> ⚠️ **Status of this document:** research reference, not legal advice. The core rules below are drawn from the federal regulation and multiple UAE legal/advisory sources and are consistent across them. The **exact wording of DET's current live form** and the **precise fine schedule** should be confirmed against the DET "Invest in Dubai" portal / a licensed registered agent at the time of filing, because authorities update forms and portals frequently. ## 1. TL;DR — the essentials

- Two separate but linked documents are involved:
  1. **Corporate Shareholding** — how the UAE company is recorded as a shareholder in the new LLC's **Memorandum of Association (MoA)**, backed by a **board resolution** of that company authorising the investment and appointing an **authorised representative/signatory**.
  2. **UBO (Real Beneficiary) Declaration** — identifying the **natural persons** who ultimately own/control ≥ 25%, filed into the company's **Real Beneficiary Register** and submitted to DET.
- Governing law today is **Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023** ("Regulating the Real Beneficiary Procedures"), effective **16 November 2023**, which **superseded Cabinet Decision No. 58 of 2020** (which had itself replaced No. 34 of 2020).
- **A company can never itself be the UBO.** You must "look through" the corporate shareholder to the individuals behind it, multiplying ownership percentages down the chain.
- Every company must keep **three registers**: (a) Register of Real Beneficiaries (UBOs), (b) Register of Partners/Shareholders, (c) Register of Nominee Directors/Board Members.
- **UBO threshold:** a natural person owning/controlling **≥ 25% of shares OR ≥ 25% of voting rights**, or control by other means (e.g. power to appoint/remove the majority of directors). Fallback: if no one qualifies, the **senior management official** is treated as the UBO.
- **Timing:** file at incorporation; **update the register and notify DET within 15 days** of any change.
- **Penalties** for non‑compliance are administered under **Cabinet Decision No. 53 of 2021** (warnings escalating to fines).

## 2. Legal framework

| Instrument | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Cabinet Decision No. 34 of 2020** | First "real beneficiary" procedures regulation | Repealed |
| **Cabinet Decision No. 58 of 2020** | Regulating the Beneficial Owner Procedures — introduced the 25% test, the registers, and filing duties | Superseded |
| **Cabinet Decision No. 53 of 2021** | Schedule of **administrative penalties** for breaching the UBO procedures | In force |
| **Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023** | Current regime — "Regulating the Real Beneficiary Procedures"; effective **16 Nov 2023**; **replaced No. 58 of 2020** | **In force** |

**What No. 109 of 2023 changed (vs 58/2020):**
- Kept the substance (25% test, the registers, the 15‑day update rule).
- Added a **risk‑based, discretionary power** for the registrar/licensing authority to determine the beneficial owner in **complex ownership structures**.
- Tightened record‑keeping, notification and AML alignment; formalised an **AML unit within the registrar's office**.
- Set an initial filing window of **60 days** from enforcement or from the date of registration for existing/new entities, with ongoing changes notified within **15 days**.

**Who it applies to / exemptions:** applies to entities licensed on the **mainland and in commercial (non‑financial) free zones**. Typically **exempt**: companies wholly owned by federal/emirate government, and entities in the **financial free zones DIFC and ADGM** (which run their own beneficial‑ownership regimes). For a **Dubai mainland LLC, the registrar is DET.**

## 3. What "UBO / Real Beneficiary" means

A **Real Beneficiary (UBO)** is the **natural person** who ultimately owns or controls the company, established through this cascade:

1. **Ownership/voting test** — any natural person who, directly or indirectly (through a chain of ownership or control), owns or controls **≥ 25% of the share capital** *or* **≥ 25% of the voting rights**.
2. **Control by other means** — if no one meets (1), or there is doubt that the person identified is the true controller, the natural person who **exercises control by other means** (e.g. the right to **appoint or dismiss the majority of the directors/managers**).
3. **Senior management fallback** — if no natural person is identified under (1) or (2), the natural person holding the position of **Senior Management Official** is deemed the UBO.

Key principle: **a legal person (a company) is never the final UBO.** Where a company sits in the ownership chain, you trace upward until you reach natural persons.

---

## 4. Tracing the UBO through a corporate shareholder (the "look‑through")

**Rule:** multiply the ownership percentages at each layer of the chain; a natural person whose *effective* (multiplied) interest reaches **≥ 25%** is a UBO.

**Worked illustration** (generic — replace with your real numbers once you send the structure):

> Assume the new LLC = **NewCo**. Shareholders: **HoldCo (UAE company) 60%**, **Individual A 20%**, **Individual B 20%**.
>
> - Individual A → **direct 20%** in NewCo → *below* 25% on direct ownership alone.
> - Individual B → **direct 20%** in NewCo → *below* 25% on direct ownership alone.
> - HoldCo → not a UBO itself; look through it. Suppose HoldCo is owned **Mr X 50% / Mr Y 50%**.
>   - Mr X effective in NewCo = 50% × 60% = **30%** → **UBO**.
>   - Mr Y effective in NewCo = 50% × 60% = **30%** → **UBO**.
> - If Individual A also owned, say, 10% of HoldCo, add the indirect slice: 20% (direct) + (10% × 60% = 6%) = **26%** → **UBO**.

**Notes:**
- Aggregate a person's **direct + all indirect** paths before applying the 25% test.
- **Nominee arrangements do not break the chain** — the natural person behind the nominee is the UBO, and any nominee director/board member must be recorded in the Nominee register.
- **Control (not just ownership)** can independently make someone a UBO even if their arithmetic percentage is below 25% (e.g. board‑appointment power, veto rights).
- For a **complex structure**, DET may exercise its **risk‑based discretion** (per 109/2023) to determine the UBO.

## 5. The three registers a company must maintain

Kept at the company's UAE registered address (usually prepared/held by the registered agent) and submitted to DET:

**A. Register of Real Beneficiaries (UBO register)** — for each UBO:
- Full name
- Nationality
- Date **and place** of birth
- Residential address (and address for notices/service)
- Passport **or** national ID / Emirates ID number, with **issue and expiry dates**, and issuing country
- **Basis** on which the person is a UBO (ownership % / voting rights / control by other means / senior management)
- **Percentage** of ownership or nature of control held
- **Date the person became** a UBO
- **Date the person ceased** to be a UBO (if applicable)

**B. Register of Partners / Shareholders** — for each shareholder (individual or corporate):
- Name (and legal name for corporate shareholders)
- Number and **class of shares** held; **voting rights**
- **Date acquired** the shareholding
- For individuals: nationality, DOB, address, passport/ID details
- For a corporate shareholder: legal form, registration/trade‑license number, registered address, and details of its legal representative

**C. Register of Nominee Directors / Board Members** — name, nationality, ID details, and the date of becoming/ceasing to act as a nominee (only if any nominee arrangement exists).

## 6. Documents DET requires at incorporation (mainland LLC with a UAE corporate shareholder)

Because the corporate shareholder is **UAE‑registered**, its documents do **not** need foreign consular attestation/legalisation (that chain only applies to foreign entities). Typical bundle:

**For the new LLC:**
- Reserved **trade name** and **initial approval** from DET
- **Memorandum of Association (MoA)** of the new LLC — notarised before the **Dubai Notary Public**, listing all shareholders (the corporate shareholder + the two individuals), share split, capital, and the manager(s)
- Tenancy/**Ejari** for the registered office
- Passport/Emirates ID/visa copies of the individual shareholders and the manager
- **UBO / Real Beneficiary Declaration** + supporting **ownership‑structure chart**

**For the UAE corporate shareholder:**
- Valid **Trade Licence**
- **Certificate of Incorporation / Registration** (as applicable)
- **Memorandum/Articles of Association** of the shareholder company
- **Board Resolution** (see §7): approving the investment/incorporation, the shareholding to be taken, and **appointing an authorised representative/signatory** to sign the MoA and incorporation papers on the company's behalf
- **Register / evidence of that company's own shareholders and UBOs** (needed to trace the look‑through)
- Passport/Emirates ID of the appointed authorised signatory
- Certificate of good standing / incumbency **[verify — sometimes requested]**

> Most of the above is assembled and submitted through the **"Invest in Dubai" (IID)** platform, in practice usually handled by a **registered agent / PRO** or a corporate‑services firm. Notarisation of the MoA and (often) the board resolution is done at a **UAE Notary Public**.

## 7. Corporate shareholding declaration — the mechanics

**a) How the UAE company is recorded as a shareholder**
- The corporate shareholder is named in the new LLC's **MoA** by its **full legal name, trade‑licence/registration number, and registered address**, with its **shareholding percentage and number/class of shares**.
- It signs the MoA and all incorporation documents **through an authorised natural person**, because a company cannot physically sign.

**b) Board resolution of the corporate shareholder (essential)**
The resolution is passed by the shareholder company's board/managers and typically must:
- Resolve to **participate in / incorporate** the new LLC and take the stated shareholding.
- Approve the **capital contribution**.
- **Appoint a named individual as authorised representative/signatory** with power to sign the MoA, open bank accounts, and complete all DET formalities.
- State the representative's **powers, and any duration/limits**.
- Be executed on company letterhead, signed by the authorised director(s), and **notarised** (UAE notary for a UAE company). If the manager appointment is stated in the MoA rather than a separate resolution, the MoA itself must be re‑notarised whenever it changes.

**c) Authorised signatory** — the individual named in the resolution appears in the incorporation file and, where relevant, on the LLC's **authorised‑signatory records**.

## 8. Standard format / mandatory fields of the UBO declaration

The declaration is a signed statement, one entry per UBO. Standard fields (consistent across DET/free‑zone forms; these mirror the register requirements in §5A):

**Company block**
- Company (trade) name; licence/registration number (if already issued); legal form; registered address.

**For each Real Beneficiary (UBO)**
1. Full legal name (as per passport)
2. Nationality (and any second nationality)
3. Date of birth
4. **Place** of birth
5. Residential address
6. Passport number + issue/expiry date + country of issue
7. Emirates ID number + expiry (if a UAE resident)
8. **Basis of beneficial ownership** — tick/select: *direct ownership ≥ 25% / indirect ownership ≥ 25% / voting rights ≥ 25% / control by other means / senior management official*
9. **Percentage owned or controlled** (state direct %, indirect %, and total effective %)
10. **Date on which the person became** a UBO
11. Date on which the person **ceased** to be a UBO (if applicable)

**Declaration & signature block**
- A statement that the information is **true, accurate and complete**, and an undertaking to notify changes within **15 days**.
- Signature of the **authorised person** (a manager/authorised signatory of the company, or the shareholders), name, capacity, date.
- Supporting attachments: **passport/ID copies of each UBO** and an **ownership‑structure chart**.

## 9. Filing, notarisation & ongoing obligations

- **Where filed:** with **DET** (the registrar for Dubai mainland), in practice via the **Invest in Dubai** platform, at incorporation.
- **Notarisation:** the **MoA** is notarised at the Dubai Notary Public; the corporate shareholder's **board resolution** is typically notarised too. The UBO declaration itself is a signed declaration (not always separately notarised, but often signed before/collected by the agent) **[verify notarisation of the UBO form specifically]**.
- **Who signs/submits:** the company's **manager/authorised signatory** (or the appointed corporate‑shareholder representative) signs and submits; a **registered agent/PRO** commonly lodges it.
- **Initial deadline:** file within **60 days** of registration (per 109/2023).
- **Ongoing:** keep all three registers current and **notify DET of any change within 15 days** — e.g. share transfer, new/departing shareholder, a person crossing the 25% threshold either way, change of manager/nominee, change to a UBO's ID details.

---

## 10. Penalties for non‑compliance (Cabinet Decision No. 53 of 2021)

- Enforcement is **escalating**: a **written warning** first, then **administrative fines**, and for continued/serious breaches, **restrictions on the board's powers** or **suspension of the licence** (commonly cited up to **one year**).
- Commonly cited fine levels: **AED 50,000** for failing to create/maintain the register or provide accurate data, **doubling to AED 100,000** on repeat, with a first‑instance notice + **15 days to rectify** in some categories. Some sources cite ranges up to **AED 1,000,000** for serious/repeated breaches. **[verify]** exact amounts against the current annexed schedule — figures vary between advisory write‑ups, and the authoritative list is the schedule annexed to Decision 53/2021 as applied by DET.

---

## 11. Practical notes

- On the mainland, this whole bundle (trade‑name, MoA notarisation, board‑resolution notarisation, UBO declaration, IID submission) is almost always run by a **registered agent / corporate‑services provider or PRO**. They also hold the statutory registers on the company's behalf.
- Since the corporate shareholder is **UAE‑registered**, you **avoid** the foreign‑document attestation chain (notarisation → MoFA in home country → UAE Embassy → MoFAIC UAE → legal translation). You still need UAE **notarisation** of the MoA/resolution.
- Have the **ownership‑structure chart** ready — DET wants to see the look‑through from the corporate shareholder to its individuals; this is what drives the UBO determination.

        [ ] Direct ownership ≥ 25%
        [ ] Indirect ownership ≥ 25% (through: ______________)
        [ ] Voting rights ≥ 25%
