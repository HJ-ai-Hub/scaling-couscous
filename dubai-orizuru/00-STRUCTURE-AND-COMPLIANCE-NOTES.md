## 3. Key compliance points / open decisions

1. **Multiple share classes** are permitted only since **Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2025** (in force 14 Oct 2025), amending Art. 76(4) of Federal Decree-Law 32/2021. Class rights **must be stated in the MoA and recorded in the Commercial Register**. Cabinet implementing rules and notary/DET practice on specific class terms are still settling — **confirm exact wording with the notary / DET / a licensed corporate lawyer before signing.**
2. **Capital must be fully paid at incorporation** (Art. 77) — partly-paid/installment shares are **not** permitted for a mainland LLC. "Staged" capital is achieved either by (Option 1) incorporating at a smaller fully-paid amount and **increasing capital to AED 200,000 in tranches**, or (Option 2) declaring AED 200,000 fully paid at incorporation and treating Podocarpus's phased cash injection as internal shareholder funding. **Decision pending — see MoA clause.**
3. **Funding by Podocarpus:** the individuals' shares are funded by Podocarpus. State the basis explicitly (equity incentive issued credited as paid, or shareholder loan repayable) so the shares are not deemed unpaid.
4. **No nominee holding.** Register the individuals directly as Class B holders. Undisclosed nominee shareholdings are unlawful in the UAE and risk invalidation/liquidation.
5. **"Absolute" control:** 80% voting gives Podocarpus ordinary + special-resolution (≥75%) control. Certain statutory minority rights remain and cannot be removed by share class.
6. **UBO filing:** submit to DET at incorporation; notify any change within **15 days**; maintain registers for the company's life + 5 years.
