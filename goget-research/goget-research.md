## 2. Founding Story & Background

GoGet was founded in **2014 in Kuala Lumpur**. The origin story is now part of Malaysian startup folklore: founder **Francesca Chia**, then ~26 and working 80–100 hour weeks as a management consultant at **Boston Consulting Group (BCG)**, hit on the idea during "mamak" (roadside eatery) conversations about work-life balance. The catalyst question was *"What if we could pay someone to run our errands?"* The team built the product over ~9 months in their spare time before going full-time.

At launch it was positioned as **Malaysia's first online marketplace for errands and deliveries**, compared to TaskRabbit, Airbnb, and MyTeksi (now Grab).

**Founding mission:** close the gap between time-poor urban professionals and an underutilised pool of people wanting flexible work — letting people escape the binary of "full-time job vs. fixed 8-hour shift." Chia's university dissertation (Northwestern, Economics, 2006–2010) was reportedly on using upskilling to reduce unemployment — a stated influence.

### Leadership

- **Francesca Chia** — Co-Founder & CEO. Ex-BCG consultant; WEF Young Global Leader (2021); active gig-policy advocate.
- **Tan Fung Wei** — Co-Founder & CTO *(also rendered "Tan Tai Fung Wei")*.
- **Muaz Jema** — Co-Founder & Financial Controller.

## 3. Business Model & Revenue Streams

GoGet runs a **commission-plus-platform-fee marketplace**, layered over time with enterprise SaaS and managed-staffing lines:

| Revenue stream | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Commission / management fee** | A management ("access") fee on business jobs — **currently 15%** (was a 10% option + prepaid plans in 2020). Unlocks dashboard, recruiting, automated deliveries, insurance, priority support. |
| **Prepaid credit packages** | Businesses buy GoGet Business Credits (no contracts); job fees + GoGetter pay are drawn down from the balance. |
| **GoGet Projects** | High-touch, account-managed staffing — GoGet handles recruitment, screening, and management. Min. 1 month, ≥5 workers/day. |
| **Cloud workforce SaaS** | Platform licensed to companies to digitise / manage their own manual workforces (announced around Series A). |
| **Delivery API** | Free to integrate; monetised via per-delivery job fees. |
| **Worker financial services (emerging)** | Stated vision to offer GoGetters savings / insurance — a potential future stream. |

> **Flag:** The 10% → 15% fee shift reflects pricing changes over time; confirm live in-app.

## 4. Services Offered

**Consumer services** (app splits jobs into Dispatch / Sales & Marketing / Part-time / Projects):

- **Delivery & dispatch** — same-day / on-demand, "deliver almost anything"
- **Food & shopping** — personal shopper buys, pays, delivers
- **Moving & household** — moving, packing, repairs, gardening, cooking, chores
- **Pet care** — dog walking, feeding, vet visits
- **Part-time / event staffing** — promoters, flyer distribution, booth crew, waiters, merchandisers, warehouse pick-and-pack, data entry, admin (30+ job types)

**Business solutions (GoGet for Business):**

- **GoGet Business** — supports gig / contract / part-time / full-time hiring; dashboard, insurance, integrations
- **GoGet Recruit** — screen 40,000+ verified GoGetters by experience, education, location, ratings; "Train & Build" a regular pool
- **GoGet Projects** — managed staffing for enterprises
- **Delivery API** — integrate online orders for fulfilment (1–2 week setup)
- **HR outsourcing** — retail, hypermarket, e-commerce, F&B, events

### How it works operationally

**Booking** → poster creates job, app auto-quotes the fee → matched with a verified GoGetter (businesses pick from applicants via Recruit). **Vetting** → ID verification + criminal background check + service-quality training + culture-fit test; ratings on every worker. **Payment** → escrow model: payment held by GoGet, disbursed to the GoGetter (cash, bank transfer, or cashless GoGet Pay / Credits → e.g. Touch 'n Go).

### Indicative pricing

- **Dispatch:** flat fee + distance + effort + timing; minimum ~RM6, roughly RM0.80–1.20/km
- **Part-time:** ~RM12/hr basic tasks, RM15–20/hr skilled; GoGetter earnings start ~RM10/hr
- **Business:** 15% management fee + prepaid credits; Projects custom-quoted

> Specific RM bands come from support-page extracts and may be outdated — GoGet auto-quotes live in-app.

## 5. Strategic Evolution — Three Phases

1. **2014 – ~2018 — Consumer errands marketplace.** Peer-to-peer errands, deliveries, dispatch, moving, grocery runs. Expanded to Penang.
2. **~2018 — Platform-as-a-Service for business.** Began offering its job-assignment / tracking tech to corporations managing distributed workforces. Chia framed this as a *new layer, not a pivot*.
3. **2020 – present — On-demand workforce / HR-tech platform.** Repositioned as an end-to-end HR system spanning **HIRE → MANAGE → PAY → TRAIN → ENGAGE** across gig / part-time / contract / full-time ("blended workforce"). COVID-19 sharply accelerated adoption.

**No formal rebrand** — it has always been "GoGet." The shift is strategic repositioning (errands → workforce HR platform), not renaming.

## 8. Competitive Landscape & Differentiation

**Differentiation:** Unlike single-vertical delivery / e-hailing players, GoGet positions itself as a **blended-workforce HR system** (hire → manage → pay → train → engage), claiming to be first in Malaysia to digitise the whole HR process for gig + part-time + contract + full-time work.

| Competitor type | Players |
|---|---|
| **Logistics / delivery** | Lalamove, Grab (GrabExpress), foodpanda, ShopeeFood |
| **Gig staffing** | FastGig, Kiddocare (childcare), Halo Delivery |
| **E-hailing (adjacent)** | Grab (dominant; ~32 e-hailing apps in MY), Bolt, inDrive |
| **Niche local** | Supahands (micro-tasks / AI labeling), Kaodim (home services) |

## 10. Recent Direction & Regulatory Context (2023–2026)

**Strategic direction:**

- Deepening the **HR-tech / blended-workforce** positioning; **GoGet Recruit** is the flagship enterprise product.
- Stated vision of "a sustainable future of work across **ASEAN**"; Chia moderated an AI panel at the **ASEAN AI Malaysia Summit 2025**. No confirmed regional operations outside Malaysia yet, and no new funding since 2020.
- **Awards:** HR Vendors of the Year — Runner-Up Best Recruitment Portal (2023), **Winner Best Recruitment Portal (2024)**.

**The central regulatory story — Gig Workers Act 2025 (Act 872):**

- Passed Dewan Rakyat **Aug 2025**, gazetted **31 Dec 2025**, **in force 31 March 2026**. Covers ~1.2–1.6M gig workers. Introduces service agreements, payment certainty, a Gig Workers Tribunal, and **mandatory PERKESO / SOCSO contributions**. Billed by the government as a "world-first" comprehensive gig-worker law.
- **GoGet was a signatory** to industry coalition statements (with Bolt, FastGig, foodpanda, Grab, Lalamove, ShopeeFood, etc.) urging consultation and impact studies before tabling.
- **Chia's public critique** (The Edge, ~Feb 2026): the SOCSO structure splits gig work into **28 job-type categories** (failing multi-task workers); no real-time system to prevent **over-contribution** beyond the ~RM13.10/month ceiling; payouts based on historical not projected income; and the "on-demand live allocation" framing fits ride-hailing better than scheduled-shift platforms like GoGet.
- **Strategic implication:** mandatory social-protection compliance *plays to GoGet's strengths* (it already built EPF / PERKESO integrations), but the 28-category structure poses real compliance burdens.
