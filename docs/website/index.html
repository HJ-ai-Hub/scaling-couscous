After-Sales & Warranty

New phones: covered by the manufacturer's warranty. Where possible we help you send the device to the brand or supplier for warranty service.

Used phones: covered by a shop warranty. The warranty period, coverage and battery terms are stated at the point of sale.

We do not provide in-house repair; for repairs we may refer you to a trusted partner workshop. Full condition, warranty scope and terms are explained before every purchase.

Returns & Exchange

Please inspect and test your device at pickup before payment is completed. Once checked and accepted, the sale is confirmed.

Dead-on-arrival / defects: if a genuine fault appears within the stated period, we will assist with repair, exchange or resolution under the applicable warranty.

Physical or liquid damage, and issues caused by misuse, are not covered. Used phones are sold in their described condition.
